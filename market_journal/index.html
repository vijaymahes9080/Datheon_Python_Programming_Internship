Architectural Highlights

Constituents CSV Ingestion

Loads resources/constituents.csv containing 500+ official S&P 500 tickers, company names, and GICS sectors. Validates symbols instantaneously via in-memory set lookups.

Transparent Pricing Model

Zero hallucinated or fabricated numbers. Positions without a manually supplied current price are marked as UNAVAILABLE, and overall totals are flagged as partial valuation.

Audit Trail CSV Export

Generates standardized, timestamped CSV records using Python's standard csv.writer, complete with calculated fields and price sourcing tags.
